City executive

The mayor exercises general supervision over city programs, services, offices, and implementation of laws and ordinances.

City council

The Sangguniang Panlungsod enacts ordinances, adopts resolutions, authorizes appropriations, and conducts legislative oversight.

129 barangays

Barangays are the city’s basic political units, each led by elected barangay officials.

Independent civic access

BetterBaguio.org organizes public information but does not speak for, represent, or transact on behalf of the City Government.
