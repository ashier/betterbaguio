What the mayor does

The mayor oversees city programs, services, and offices, and is responsible for carrying out laws and ordinances.

What the council does

The Sangguniang Panlungsod passes ordinances and resolutions, approves appropriations, and reviews how city policies are carried out.

Your barangay

Baguio has 129 barangays, each with officials elected by the people who live there.

What BetterBaguio does

We make public information easier to find, but we do not represent the City Government or process transactions for it.
